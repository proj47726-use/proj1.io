Browser history · doubly linked list

What it is. A chain where every page knows the page before it and the page after it.
Why. Back and Forward just move one pointer (O(1)). Opening a new page in the middle cuts the chain, so the forward pages are gone. Click any page to jump to it.

Closed tabs · stack (LIFO)

What it is. A pile of plates: the last tab you closed is the first one you get back, with all of its history.

Downloads · queue (FIFO)

What it is. A line at a ticket counter: first in, first served.
Press ⬇ Download to join the line, then process the oldest one.

Unique domains · set

What it is. A collection that keeps each website once, however many times you visit it.

Bookmarks · tree

What it is. Folders that hold links and other folders. Click a folder to choose where the next bookmark goes.
